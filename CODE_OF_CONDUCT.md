# Code of conduct

Voice Tutor is made for people learning a language, and many of the people around the project write in a language that is not their first one. Be patient and kind about language mistakes, in issues and in code reviews alike.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person; explain your reasons.
- Write in English, Spanish or Portuguese; maintainers may use a translation tool to answer.
- Respect privacy: never post someone else's personal data, keys or recordings.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or sexualized language and images.
- Mocking someone's accent, grammar or level of English.
- Publishing private messages or personal information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive behaviour after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules and may block people who keep breaking them. Report a problem privately to the maintainers through GitHub (the contact on the repository's profile, or a private vulnerability report if nothing else is available). Reports are handled confidentially.

This text is the project's own; it follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
